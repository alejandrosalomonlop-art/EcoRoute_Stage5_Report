# EcoRoute – Project Focus

## 1. Selected Sector & Focus Area

Our project will focus on optimizing transport routes for urgent laboratory samples, blood supplies and pharmaceuticals between hospitals and clinics.

## 2. Target Problem Statement

Medical logistics require high precision, strict temperature control and a rapid speed across dense urban networks. A non-optimized route leads to excessive fuel consumption and CO₂ emissions, increasing operational costs, while delivery delays can compromise patient care and emergency supplies.

## 3. Proposed Fleet Profile

The fleet will mainly use electric motorcycles with insulated or refrigerated boxes for urgent deliveries, and specialized small electric vehicles with temperature-controlled compartments for larger transport. In this way, we ensure a rapid response, low urban emissions and a secure medical transport.
